I am an M.S. student advised by Prof. [Seong Jae Hwang](https://micv.yonsei.ac.kr/seongjae) at [MICV Lab](https://micv.yonsei.ac.kr/home).

My research focuses on aligning brain and AI representations—currently exploring multimodal alignment and brain decoding. Going forward, I am interested in subject-invariant learning and incorporating domain-specific structure into self-supervised methods.
